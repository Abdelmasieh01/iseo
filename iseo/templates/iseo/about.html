About the ISEO

Facilitating global academic experiences for Kuwait University students since 2010.

Our Mission

The International Student Exchange Office (ISEO) is a unit within the Department of Information Systems and Operations Management (ISOM), College of Business Administration, Kuwait University. Our mission is to broaden the academic and cultural horizons of CBA students by facilitating semester-long exchange experiences at our partner universities worldwide.

What We Do

We manage all aspects of the student exchange lifecycle — from initial eligibility assessment through post-exchange credit transfer support. Our team coordinates directly with partner institutions to maintain available exchange slots, track application deadlines, and ensure a smooth transition for outbound students.

This website serves as the central hub for all exchange-related information and the primary channel through which students submit and track their applications. It was developed as part of the ISOM 331 Systems Analysis and Design course (Spring 2026) following a rigorous requirements analysis process.

System Requirements Context

This platform was built upon requirements gathered through four methodologies: benchmarking of Cornell University and FIU international exchange portals, user scenario analysis across three user roles, secondary research from Google Scholar and IEEE Xplore on student information systems, and heuristic evaluation using Nielsen's 10 usability principles.

Key requirements driving this implementation include: online multi-step application forms with inline validation, a filterable partner university directory, real-time application status tracking by reference number, automated reference number assignment, and a contact channel for direct advisor communication.
