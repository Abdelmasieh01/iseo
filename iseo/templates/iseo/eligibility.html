Eligibility Criteria

Check whether you qualify for the International Student Exchange Program.

🎓

Academic Standing

  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 on a 4.0 scale
  • Some universities require 3.0 or higher — check the partner directory
  • Completed at least 60 credit hours by exchange semester
  • Currently enrolled as a full-time student at CBA
📋

Enrollment Status

  • Must be an undergraduate or graduate CBA student
  • In good disciplinary standing with the university
  • No outstanding financial obligations to the university
  • Approval from your department academic advisor
🌐

Language Requirements

  • English-medium programs: TOEFL iBT ≥ 79 or IELTS ≥ 6.0
  • Top-ranked destinations may require TOEFL iBT 100+ or IELTS 7.5
  • French/Spanish/German-medium: B2 proficiency minimum
  • Language certificates must be less than 2 years old
📄

Required Documents

  • Valid passport (not expiring within 6 months of return)
  • Official university transcript
  • Language proficiency certificate
  • One academic recommendation letter
  • Personal statement (150–500 words)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I go on exchange in my first year?

No. You must have completed a minimum of 60 credit hours, which typically means you are at least in your third year of study.

How many semesters can I go on exchange?

The standard exchange is one semester. Extended exchanges of two consecutive semesters may be approved on a case-by-case basis.

Will my courses count toward my Kuwait University degree?

Yes, subject to advance credit approval from your department. You must submit a study plan before departure and get written approval from your academic advisor.

What if my first-choice university is full?

Your second choice university will be considered automatically. If both are unavailable, the ISEO will contact you to discuss alternatives before making a final decision.

Is there a financial grant for exchange students?

Exchange students continue to pay Kuwait University tuition and are exempt from tuition at the host university. Limited travel grants may be available — contact the ISEO for current funding opportunities.
